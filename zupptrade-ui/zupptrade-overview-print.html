A direction only becomes a trade if it survives the gauntlet.

The bias is turned into a specific options spread, then forced through four quality gates. Fail any hard gate and the trade is rejected — standing aside is a valid outcome.

After entry

The trade watches itself.

A live position is never place-and-forget. It's re-checked on a schedule through the session, and each check picks one of four actions — with exit levels that tighten as expiry approaches.

Action

Hold

The trade is behaving. Nothing to do — keep letting time work in its favour.

Action

Watch

The market is approaching a level of interest. Probability is re-checked more closely.

Action

Readjust

Pressure is building. The position can be rolled or tightened to defend it.

Action

Exit

A defence line is breached. All legs are closed — decisively, by rule.

Volatility spike → immediate exit

A sharp intraday jump in volatility forces the position closed regardless of price.

Extreme volatility → pause & alert

When conditions turn extreme, automated monitoring pauses and hands the decision to you.

Control & verification

Three ways the system keeps you in command.

Audit

Every trade is recorded end to end — the signal that started it, the trade built, the fills paid, and every check that followed.

  • How often the approach wins
  • How much of the possible profit it kept
  • Whether the signal was actually right

Critical alerts

Whenever the system can't be certain your position is safe, it stops and shows the full picture rather than guessing.

  • An order leg didn't fill — the other is auto-closed
  • An exit couldn't complete, or fills came in worse than expected
  • Each alert stays until you acknowledge it

Simulation

Replay a full multi-day trade in minutes against saved market data — touching no live market and placing no real orders.

  • The whole flow runs: signal, trade, monitoring, exit
  • Verify exactly how a setup behaves
  • Tweak the scenario and re-run "what-ifs" before trusting it live
