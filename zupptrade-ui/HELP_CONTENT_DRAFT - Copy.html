Edit freely; once you're happy I'll wire it into a header **?** modal. --- ## What ZuppTrade is *(the 2-line philosophy)* **An automated Nifty 50 trading assistant — covering both weekly options and logical intraday futures — built for consistency, not big wins; every trade is rule-based, repeatable, and sized to your risk.** **It doesn't stop at entry: positions are monitored automatically and exited by rule — and nothing is a black box, so each signal, trade, and exit traces back to the exact inputs and simple maths you can re-derive yourself.** --- ## Trading tab — generate a signal, get a trade, let it run *What you do here:* - **Your view (optional):** type a market view or paste commentary — it feeds one part of the scoring. - **Generate Signal:** the system reads the market and returns a **direction**. - **Trade card:** that direction becomes a specific options spread, sized to your risk. *How the signal is calculated — one line each:* - Five groups of evidence ("tiers") each cast a simple **bullish / neutral / bearish** vote. - The tiers: **price structure · technical indicators · institutional flow (FII/DII) · volatility & macro · commentary & news** — some count more than others. - Their weighted result becomes your **bias** (Bullish / Neutral / Bearish) and **strength** (Weak / Mild / Extreme). - **Confidence** reflects how many tiers agree, adjusted for how calm or stressed volatility is. - When volatility is **extreme, the system won't auto-trade** — it flags the situation to you instead. *How the trade is built — one line each:* - The **strategy** is chosen from your bias, the volatility regime, and how rich options are priced. - The system estimates **how far the market can realistically move** by expiry, and sells strikes beyond that likely range. - The candidate must clear a series of **quality gates** — a high probability of profit, internal consistency, and a worthwhile return — or it's rejected. - **Position size** is set so a realistic bad outcome stays within a small, fixed slice of your capital. *Live Trades Monitor (the right-hand panel) — the trade watches itself:* - A live trade is **not place-and-forget**: it's re-checked regularly while the market is open. - Each check picks one of four actions: **Hold · Watch · Readjust · Exit**. - Exit levels **tighten as expiry nears** — Watch as the market approaches, Readjust as it gets closer, Exit if it breaches. - **Safety first:** a sharp volatility spike forces an immediate exit; extreme volatility pauses monitoring and alerts you. --- ## Futures tab — a separate, disciplined intraday engine - A standalone **intraday Nifty-futures** strategy: intraday only, hard stop-loss, no overnight risk. - Runs **twice around the open** — first a primed plan, then a confirmation of which setup is live. - Uses **yesterday's range** to draw reference levels and a grid of long/short rotation and breakout/breakdown setups. - **Bias picks the side**, **confidence sets how aggressively**, and a **compression check can veto** a trade. - A trade is placed **only when price actually reaches the trigger level** — no anticipation, no chasing. - Every trade carries **both a stop-loss and a profit-booking target, set upfront** — disciplined risk management, never improvised. --- ## Audit tab — every number is re-derivable - Each trade is recorded **end to end**: the signal that started it, the trade built, the fills paid, and every check that followed. - Turned into plain figures you can re-derive yourself: **how often it wins**, **how much of the possible profit it kept**, and **whether the signal was right**. --- ## Profile tab — your risk settings - Your **capital** and risk limits — how safe a trade must be, how much you'll risk, and how tightly the trade must hold together. - The **tier weights** that personalise how your signal is scored. --- ## Critical Alerts — when the system stops and asks you - A **Critical Alert** is raised whenever the system **can't be certain your position is safe** — it stops and shows the full picture rather than guessing. - Typical triggers: an order leg didn't fill (the other leg is auto-closed), an exit couldn't complete, extreme volatility forced an emergency action, or fills came in worse than expected. - Each alert **stays until you acknowledge it**, so nothing critical is ever silently missed. --- ## Simulation mode — test a full trade safely - A **testing mode**: instead of live market data, the system reads a saved **scenario** (a market path, option chain, flows, and sentiment). - Start it with a scenario and the **whole flow — signal, trade, monitoring, exit — runs from that saved data**, touching no live market and placing no real orders. - Lets you **replay a full multi-day trade in minutes** to verify how it behaves; copy a scenario, tweak the data, and re-run to explore new "what-ifs" before trusting it live. --- ### Ready to wire in All three of your calls are applied: Monitor is a **sub-section under Trading**, **no hard numbers**, and **Critical Alerts + Simulation** are included.
